# Ungaikyo obsidian Overseer

Ungaikyo now uses the vanilla Overseer hull and Type250 armament, with Cautious boss AI. Its appearance follows vanilla THREAT machinery: repeated interlocking plates, spars, radial assemblies and recessed hardware, with charcoal obsidian material and a restrained gray-blue sheen. The user clarified that THREAT is not living; its seemingly organic forms result from evolutionary distributed logic. The polished chrome candidate and the biological carapace candidate were both rejected.

The skin inherits native Overseer geometry, statistics and engine/weapon slots. The runtime sprite keeps the native 148 x 160 canvas; the hull center is (74,74) from the bottom-left, equivalent to PNG pivot (74,86). The generated artwork is fitted to the native visible footprint. It is an artistic reskin, not a change to the collision polygon or slots.

Keep the unique hull ID `chief_navigator_ungaikyo` and historical variant ID `chief_navigator_ungaikyo_Fabricator`. The latter is a compatibility identifier, not the current hull class. Preserve the Reflection Swarm/extraction system, five initial escorts, reflection selection/resurrection/collapse, quest identity, music and rewards. The boss remains Cautious; mirrors retain their existing Fearless configuration. Existing saved fleet objects are not recreated or replenished.

There is no new shader, framebuffer capture or custom reflection renderer. The sprite carries the restrained sheen in its artwork. When enabled, GraphicsLib automatically inherits native Overseer material/normal/surface maps through its base-hull fallback. Keep the native `threat` classification and actual Fragment Swarm mechanics; the tag suppresses the vanilla player-retrofit visual overlay without removing the swarm.

Combat-only mirrors enter at their native maximum CR. Fresh detached members have no crew or AI campaign fleet, so the native crew modifier previously reduced a normal 70% maximum to 20%; filling that reduced maximum preserved the bug. The generated members now receive their own minimum crew and refresh stats before CR initialization, including live owned modules at construction completion. Automated hulls retain their native zero-crew requirements and CR modifiers. This uses no player crew or source-member changes and does not replenish CR during normal combat.

Inputs were an 8x nearest-neighbor enlargement of the installed vanilla `graphics/ships/threat/overseer.png` as the exact silhouette/layout reference, and vanilla `graphics/ships/threat/fabricator.png` as the THREAT machinery/style reference. Image 2 is vanilla Fabricator, supporting reference for THREAT's established alien MACHINE construction and hand-painted sprite detail. THREAT is self-organizing distributed machinery, not a living creature. It only SUGGESTS organic form through repeated, irregularly nested mechanical elements. Preserve image 1's existing hard mechanical geometry faithfully rather than redesigning it. Keep its sharp stepped interlocking plates, ribbed assemblies, thin structural spars, layered wedge-shaped blocks, radial socket clusters, and complex recessed hardware. Those are evolved alien machine structures. Keep the exact Overseer silhouette, appendages, weapon socket centers, engine positions, orientation and canvas proportions. Change ONLY the surface material and restrained color treatment: charcoal-black obsidian-like dark ceramic machinery, subtle low-contrast gray-blue sheen on a FEW existing larger plates, dark gunmetal mechanisms, muted brown-gray joints and faint small scuffs. Match vanilla THREAT's muted contrast and painted gritty sprite finish. Strongest edge highlights should be medium gray, not white or silver; most plates should look rough or satin. Reflection is a subdued suggestion, not a glossy chrome coat. Keep enough gray separation to read against black space.

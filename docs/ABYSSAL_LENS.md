# Abyssal Focusing Lens

Recovering all six original Ithaca defense components reveals one additional native derelict research station in Sanghata orbit, Avici. Carried and previously installed components qualify; opening a salvage screen or using the maximum-upgrade testing override does not. The station drops one Abyssal Focusing Lens and ordinary non-core salvage. Labor II still requires four distinct installed components.

Deliver the Lens to Menelaus to consume and permanently install it. Eligible Ithaca naval beams retain their native white core, collision and damage while their fringe stays teal near the muzzle, blends smoothly to red by halfway along the actual ray, and retains the native Abyssal Glare impact explosions. Unupgraded and hostile Wall beams retain their existing behavior.

The original six item IDs and component bits remain unchanged. A durable recovered mask and one-time introduction flag are additive save state. The new station event latches before creation, respects existing ID claims and missing authored worlds, and never respawns a salvaged, missing or previously attempted station.

Focused verification sources: `AbyssalLensRegression`, `AviciResearchStationRegression` and `AbyssalBeamGradientRenderRegression`.

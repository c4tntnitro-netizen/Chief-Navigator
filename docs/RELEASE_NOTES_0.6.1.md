# Chief Navigator 0.6.1

November 14, 2026. Budai fixes for Starsector 0.98.5a-RC4.

- Clear Budai's lingering impulse shader and field movement penalties when he dies, becomes a hulk or leaves combat. Cleanup runs independently of his hullmod callbacks and also works while paused.
- Restore the stock Shrouded Maw's piloting and ordinary Convulsive Lunge decisions, including backward lunges. Remove Budai's forced Fearless settings and scripted retreat suppression.
- Retain his four-lunge enrage sequence, twelve-second system cooldown, field expansion, damage rules, music and salvage rewards.

Replace the previous Chief Navigator folder with this package and restart Starsector. Existing quest progress is retained.

Requirements remain Peerless Skills 0.1 or newer, GraphicsLib 1.10.2 or newer, and Combat Chatter 1.15.0 or newer. LunaLib and Hall of Triumph remain optional.

For the preceding expedition update, see the [0.6.0 cumulative notes](RELEASE_NOTES_0.6.0.md).

# Chief Navigator 0.5.0

An installable release of Chief Navigator for Starsector 0.98a, including the
Sinni expedition, Orion Knot systems, FOB Ithaca, five Labors and post-Labor hunts.

## Recent changes

- Budai uses Fearless AI and refuses retreat orders and fleet withdrawal. His
  half-hull lunge phase produces heavy smoke, followed by a five-second expansion
  of the distortion bubble as smoke emission returns to normal.
- Budai has no passive hull regeneration and 30% less hull than the previous
  boss version. His bubble reduces eligible outside-origin damage by 30% and
  displays `DEFLECTED!`. All lunges retain the native activation phases and
  twelve-second cooldown.
- All of Sanzu has nebula-like reduced sensor range.
- Hammer and Atropos batteries use a tighter spread. Ithaca's mirrored naval
  mounts and Cloudswarm arrays align with their intended sockets.
- Labor V's gate defense stations suppress repeated damage flashes, and both
  reserve stations rotate to expose their modules normally.
- The Labor V ending cuts music immediately, waits ten seconds, then plays
  PeriTune's *Glistening Ripples*.
- Aias Kleon uses vanilla portrait35. Includes the saved Starving Fabricator
  ship edits and the corrected Budai salvage dialogue.
- Complete music credits, a standard MIT license for original project material,
  and separate third-party notices are included.

## Installation

1. Download **Chief-Navigator-0.5.0.zip** from the release assets.
2. Extract its **Chief Navigator** folder into **Starsector/mods**. Replace an
   older Chief Navigator folder when updating.
3. Install **GraphicsLib 1.10.2 or newer** and **Combat Chatter 1.15.0 or newer**.
4. Enable Chief Navigator and its dependencies in the launcher, then restart
   Starsector.

The ZIP includes the compiled jar and required runtime assets. GitHub's automatic
source archives contain the development repository, including retained art and
audio source material; use the named release ZIP for installation. Dependencies
and vanilla game assets are supplied by their respective installations.

# Audio sources

`omega_time_freeze.wav` and `omega_time_freeze_charge.wav` are original,
procedurally synthesized effects. They contain no recordings, samples, or other
third-party audio. The reproducible source is
`tools/generate_time_freeze_audio.ps1`; both effects may be used, modified, and
redistributed with the mod without attribution.

`budai_enrage_screech.wav` is likewise an original procedural synthesis with
no recordings, samples, or third-party audio. Its reproducible source is
`tools/generate_budai_screech.ps1`, and it may be used, modified, and
redistributed with the mod without attribution.

`sounds/music/chief_navigator_alpha_odyssey_exploration.ogg` is **Rising Up**
by **Ryini Beats**. The artist marks the track free for noncommercial use with
credit. Chief Navigator uses it as Alpha Odyssey's exploration music and does
not claim ownership of the composition or recording.

- Credit: Prod. Ryini Beats

`sounds/music/chief_navigator_budai_battle.ogg` is **Heart of Corruption**
by **Lappy**, used as Budai's dedicated battle theme and as the cue for
Gautama reincarnating in any ordinary battle. Labor V's perimeter battles use
**Abyssal Rhapsody**, and its center battle retains vocal **My Gospel is
Gunpowder** instead. Chief
Navigator does not claim ownership of the composition or recording.

- Credit: Music by Lappy
- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gBaNsd3Ycc8
- Release date listed by the distributor: 2024-01-26

`sounds/music/KARMA_1Loop.mp3`, `KARMA_Loop_A.mp3`, and
`KARMA_Loop_B.mp3` are retained from Avici's former campaign-music sequence,
but are no longer played there. The supplied files identify the artist as
**zippy**. Avici now uses the vanilla `music_dweller_encounter_hostile` cue
directly from Starsector, without copying or overriding its audio asset.

- Credit: Music by zippy
- Supplied file metadata date: 2025

not claim ownership of the composition or recording.

- Credit: Music by Lappy
- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vLWEkvpPBDY

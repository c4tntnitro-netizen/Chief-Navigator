## Starsector and other third-party assets

Starsector and its original code, music, hulls, weapons, portraits, sprites and
other assets belong to [Fractal Softworks](https://fractalsoftworks.com/) and
their respective creators. Chief Navigator references game-provided assets
and includes adaptations of game artwork/data; those underlying materials
remain outside the MIT grant. Avici's vanilla Shrouded Dweller music is
referenced from the installed game and is not copied into this repository.

The game's license and applicable modding permissions continue to apply.
GraphicsLib, Combat Chatter, Console Commands and any other external libraries
retain their own licenses; importing their APIs does not relicense them.

The original procedural effects generated by
`tools/generate_time_freeze_audio.ps1` and `tools/generate_budai_screech.ps1`
are covered by the project's MIT grant. Supplied recordings and assets whose
provenance or licensing is not recorded are not represented as MIT-licensed
original work. The supplied Budai screech MP3, its converted runtime WAV and
naval gun sound recordings are excluded from the MIT grant; this document
does not grant rights held by their original creators.

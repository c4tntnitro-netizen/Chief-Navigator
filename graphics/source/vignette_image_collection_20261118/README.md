# Vignette image collection — 18 November 2026

Ten downloaded image candidates for Chief Navigator’s remaining active stellar vignettes. Open [the offline gallery](index.html) to compare them. All source images were visually inspected and retain the original downloaded bytes. The full-resolution renditions range from 720×486 to 5400×3600; their hashes, dimensions, source pages and direct download URLs are in [manifest.json](manifest.json).

This is a source collection, not a runtime change. Nebula already has its selected artwork. Giant and Massive share a progress category but have different scenes, so this collection includes ordinary giant, red-supergiant and blue-supergiant imagery. Brown Dwarf and Last Request are retired and are not included; bespoke regional arrivals are outside this stellar-image collection.

## Reuse and attribution

- **NASA SVS (01–03):** [archive policy](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/) identifies its content as public domain unless an exception is stated. No exception was found for these selected stills. Keep the supplied NASA/creator credit in the game and distribution documentation.
- **ESO / ESA-Hubble (05–09):** [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), confirmed by the publishers’ [ESO terms](https://www.eso.org/public/outreach/copyright/) and [ESA/Hubble terms](https://esahubble.org/copyright/). Display the full, unaltered credit visibly alongside the image; include source and license links in the distributed credits. Identify subsequent adaptations, such as a crop or recoloring. The license permits redistribution and adaptation, including commercial use, subject to attribution.
- **NASA media guidelines (04 and 10):** [NASA’s usage policy](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) supports informational use with acknowledgment, including image collections and graphical simulations. These are recorded under that policy, rather than asserted to have a CC0 license. No separate third-party copyright restriction was found on the selected source pages.

These source images retain their own terms; the mod’s code license does not relicense them. No agency endorsement is implied. The originals have no added logos or people. The gallery scales full frames for display and leaves all source files unchanged. If integrating an image later, put its credit below the in-game visual and carry its source/license information into the mod’s third-party notices.

## Selection and verification

The edge-on black-hole still is preferred over the initially downloaded overhead view because the disk arcs above and below the shadow are visible. Antares and the blue-supergiant illustration are particularly direct matches for their branches. The giant surface image is observational and deliberately low-detail; the engulfment alternative supplies an explanatory sequence. The trinary panel is a scientific model/observation comparison, not a clean cinematic view of three suns.

All ten files pass image decoding and SHA-256 checks. Sources and publisher policies were retrieved on 18 November 2026. Research snapshots and collection scripts are retained locally under `out_vignette_image_collection_20261118`; they are not runtime assets or a release package. No JAR, dialogue, game setting or save was changed for this collection.
